Sounds of Uzbekistan

Solo live sets blending bass guitar, bowed textures, electronics, visuals and ethnic influences. Real sounds, locations and stories from Uzbekistan turned into electronic compositions and live audiovisual shows.

  • Creator and composer of the “Sounds of Uzbekistan” audiovisual project
  • Co-founder of the “NeoForMusic” label
  • Sadowsky artist and ambassador
  • DR Strings artist and ambassador
  • Rootz artist and ambassador
