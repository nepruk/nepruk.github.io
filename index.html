About

Bass. Bow. Beats.

NepruK is a live electronic artist from Central Asia blending bass guitar, electronics and visual performance.

Her live sets combine cinematic electronic sound, improvisation and energetic bass-driven performance, creating a distinctive audiovisual experience shaped by contemporary electronic music and regional influences.

Her work explores the intersection of contemporary electronic music, live bass performance and regional cultural influences from Central Asia.

Audiovisual project

Sounds of Uzbekistan

Alongside solo performances, NepruK is the creator of “Sounds of Uzbekistan” — an audiovisual project transforming real sounds, locations and stories from Uzbekistan into modern electronic compositions and live performances.
